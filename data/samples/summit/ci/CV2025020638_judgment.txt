This matter is before the Court upon the Motion for Default Judgment of the Plaintiff,
PennyMac Loan Services, LLC, to obtain a finding of default upon the Note as described in the
Complaint; and to foreclose the lien of the Mortgage securing the obligation of the Note upon the
real estate described hereto; and to require all parties to set up their claims to the real estate or be
forever barred.
The Court finds that all necessary parties are properly before the Court; that the following
Defendants have been duly served with Summons and a copy of the Complaint, or by publication
with proof of the last date of publication filed with the Court, to wit:
• Melissa Ward Kiernan by Certified Mail on July 6, 2025
• Henry Cleveland by Publication on November 3, 2025
The Court finds that pursuant to Rule 55(A), that the Defendants, Melissa Ward Kiernan and
Henry Cleveland are in default for failure to answer or otherwise respond to the Plaintiff's
Complaint and have thereby confessed the allegations of the Complaint to be true, and said
Defendants are forever barred from asserting any right, title or interest in and to the hereinafter
described premises.
The Court further finds that the doctrine of Lis Pendens has attached to the real property,
which is the subject of this action on February 11, 2025. Accordingly, all liens and interests filed
for record after February 11, 2025, and all pending actions are forever barred.

The Court finds that the allegations contained in the Complaint are true, that there is due and
owing to the Plaintiff, on the Note, the principal balance of $158,800.26 plus interest at the rate of
2.99000 percent per annum from April 1, 2022 together with late charges, advances for the
protection and maintenance of the property, condominium association or homeowners association
dues and penalties and costs.
The Court finds that the Note was secured by the Mortgage held by Plaintiff which, except
for property taxes or assessments, constitutes a valid and subsisting lien upon the subject property
commonly known as 1764 Graham Rd, Silver Lake, OH 44224, and more particularly described in
Exhibit ‘A’ hereto. The Court further finds that the subject mortgage was recorded on October 20,
2020, in Instrument 56584075, of the records of the Summit County Recorder; that the Mortgage
was subsequently assigned to Plaintiff and that the conditions of said Mortgage have been broken
and the Plaintiff is entitled to have the equity of redemption of the Defendant-titleholders
foreclosed.
The Court finds that the Summit County Treasurer has or may have an interest in the subject
property, the exact amount which cannot be ascertained at this time, but will be known upon the
sale of the subject property. This interest is the first and best lien upon the subject property, senior
in priority to all other liens, and will be protected accordingly.
IT IS THEREFORE ORDERED, ADJUDGED AND DECREED
Plaintiff is entitled to a finding of default upon the Note and judgment for the sums set forth
above.
Plaintiff is entitled to a decree of foreclosure on the Mortgage filed October 20, 2020, in
Instrument 56584075, of the records of the Summit County Recorder. Unless there is paid or
caused to be paid by the Defendant(s) within five (5) days of the date of this entry: (i) to the Clerk
of this Court the costs of this action; and (ii) to the Plaintiff, the sums found to be due and owing to
the Plaintiff as set forth above, the equity of redemption of any and all defendants and all persons
