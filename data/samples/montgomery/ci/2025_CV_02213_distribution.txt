This matter came to be heard on the return of the Sheriff of this County, for the sale of the subject real
property on July 10, 2026, for $173,100.00, to JA Realty Investments III, LLC (hereinafter “grantee”); and the
Court finding that the sale was made in conformity with the law and orders of this Court, hereby orders the sale
in these proceedings approved and confirmed.

the real estate to the extent necessary for the protection of its title, and a writ of possession, is hereby awarded to
put the grantee in possession of the subject real property.
