This matter is before the Court upon the Motion for Default Judgment of the Plaintiff,
Nationstar Mortgage LLC, to obtain a finding of default upon the Note as described in the
Complaint; and to foreclose the lien of the Mortgage securing the obligation of the Note upon the
real estate described hereto; and to require all parties to set up their claims to the real estate or be
forever barred.
The Court finds that all necessary parties are properly before the Court; that the following
Defendants have been duly served with Summons and a copy of the Complaint, or by publication
with proof of the last date of publication filed with the Court, to wit:
 Christopher M Bridewell by Certified Mail on April 12, 2025
 Montgomery County Treasurer by Certified Mail on April 16, 2025
 Unknown Spouse, if any of Christopher M Bridewell by Certified Mail on April
12, 2025
The Court finds that pursuant to Rule 55(A), that the Defendants, Christopher M
Bridewell and Unknown Spouse, if any of Christopher M Bridewell are in default for failure to
answer or otherwise respond to the Plaintiff's Complaint and have thereby confessed the
allegations of the Complaint to be true, and said Defendants are forever barred from asserting
any right, title or interest in and to the hereinafter described premises.
The Court further finds that the doctrine of Lis Pendens has attached to the real property,
which is the subject of this action on April 9, 2025. Accordingly, all liens and interests filed for
record after April 9, 2025, and all pending actions are forever barred.
The Court finds that the allegations contained in the Complaint are true, that there is due
and owing to the Plaintiff, from the Defendant, Christopher M Bridewell, on the Note, the
principal balance of $158,051.96 plus interest at the rate of 3.25000 percent per annum from
May 1, 2022 together with late charges, advances for the protection and maintenance of the
property, and costs.
The Court finds that the Note was secured by the Mortgage held by Plaintiff which,
except for property taxes or assessments, constitutes a valid and subsisting lien upon the subject
property commonly known as 1231 Marsha Dr, Miamisburg, OH 45342, and more particularly
described in Exhibit ‘A’ hereto. The Court further finds that the subject mortgage was recorded
on September 28, 2021, in Instrument 2021-00074023, of the records of the Montgomery County
Recorder; that the Mortgage was subsequently assigned to Plaintiff and that the conditions of
said Mortgage have been broken and the Plaintiff is entitled to have the equity of redemption of
the Defendant-titleholders foreclosed.
The Court finds that the Defendant, Montgomery County Treasurer, has filed an answer
or appearance herein, and has or may have an interest in the subject property, the exact amount
which cannot be ascertained at this time, but will be known upon the sale of the subject property.
This interest is the first and best lien upon the subject property, senior in priority to all other
liens, and will be protected accordingly.
IT IS THEREFORE ORDERED, ADJUDGED AND DECREED
Plaintiff is entitled to a finding of default upon the Note and judgment for the sums set
forth above.
Plaintiff is entitled to a decree of foreclosure on the Mortgage filed September 28, 2021,
in Instrument 2021-00074023, of the records of the Montgomery County Recorder. Unless there
is paid or caused to be paid by the Defendants within five (5) days of the date of this entry: (i) to
the Clerk of this Court the costs of this action; and (ii) to the Plaintiff, the sums found to be due
and owing to the Plaintiff as set forth above, the equity of redemption of any and all defendants
and all persons claiming under and through them in said Real Property are forever barred and
foreclosed; and an Order of Sale shall issue to the Sheriff of this County, directing said Sheriff to
appraise, advertise and sell said real property according to the law and orders of this Court, and
to report the results of the sale proceedings to this Court.
That the Sheriff shall send counsel for the party requesting the Order of Sale a copy of the
publication notice promptly upon its first publication.
That the Sheriff shall pay from the proceeds of said sale, upon the claims herein found,
the amounts thereof in the following order of priority:
1. To the Clerk of this Court, the costs of this action including the fees of appraisers.
2. To the Treasurer of this County, the taxes and assessments, due and owing as of the
date of transfer of the property after Sheriff’s Sale.
3. To the Plaintiff, the sum of $158,051.96 plus interest at the rate of 3.25000 percent per
annum from May 1, 2022, together with all expenses and costs.
4. The balance of the sale proceeds, if any shall be held by the Sheriff to await further
Order of this Court.
THIS IS A FINAL APPEALABLE ORDER. PURSUANT TO APP.R.4, THESE
PARTIES SHALL FILE A NOTICE OF APPEAL WITHIN THIRTY (30) DAYS.
To the Clerk of Courts:
Pursuant to Civ. R.58(B), please serve the attorney for each party and each party not
represented by counsel with Notice of Judgment and its date of entry upon the journal.
The Court finds there is no just reason for delay.
IT IS SO ORDERED.
