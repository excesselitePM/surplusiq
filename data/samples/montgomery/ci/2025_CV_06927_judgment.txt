THIS CAUSE was submitted to the court and heard upon the Complaint for Foreclosure
and Motion for Default Judgment of the Plaintiff. The Court finds that all necessary parties have
been served with Summons according to laws and are properly before the Court; that the
Defendant(s), Unknown Heirs, Devisees, Legatees, Executors, Administrators, Spouses, and
Assigns of James E. Caldwell, Unknown Spouse of James E. Caldwell, Kelly Heart, Unknown
Spouse of Kelly Heart, Diane Liles, Unknown Spouse of Diane Liles, Iona Lockwood, Unknown
Spouse of Iona Lockwood, Marilyn Moffett, Unknown Spouse of Marilyn Moffett, and United
States of America, Secretary of Housing and Urban Development are in default of Answer or other
pleading and thereby confesses the allegations of the Complaint to be true, and said Defendant is
forever barred from asserting any right, title or interest in and to the hereinafter described premises.
The Court finds that there is due the Treasurer of Montgomery County, Ohio, taxes, accrued
taxes, assessments and penalties on the premises hereinafter described, as shown on the County
Treasurer’s tax duplicate, the exact amount being unascertainable at the present time, but which
amount will be ascertainable in accordance with Ohio Revised Code Section 323.47; which are a
valid and subsisting first lien thereon for that amount so owing.
The Court finds on the evidence adduced that there is due the Plaintiff on the promissory
note (the “Note”) as set forth in Count One of Plaintiff’s Complaint, the outstanding principal
balance of $69,711.60, plus interest on the outstanding principal balance at the rate of 5.0 percent
per annum from June 1, 2025 until fully paid, plus late charges, advances made for the payment of
taxes, assessments, insurance premiums, costs incurred for the protection of the Property under
Section 5301.233 of the Ohio Revised Code and court costs recoverable for this civil action, for
which sum judgment is hereby rendered in favor of the Plaintiff.
The Court further finds that in order to secure the payment of the Note, James E. Caldwell,
executed and delivered to Plaintiff a certain mortgage executed simultaneously with the Note (the
“Mortgage”) as in Paragraph Two of Plaintiff’s Complaint described, thereby conveying to it the
property described in the Complaint as follows:
Situate in the State of Ohio, County of Montgomery, City of Huber Heights and
being Lot Numbered 12707 Herbert C. Huber Plat No. 53, Section 1, as recorded
in Plat Book 99, Page 28 of the Plat Records of Montgomery County, Ohio.
Parcel Numbers: P70-00407-0005
Address: 4777 Taylorsville Road, Huber Heights, OH 45424 (“Property”)
That the Mortgage was duly recorded with the Recorder of Montgomery County on January
9, 2020 Instrument No. 2020-00001534, and thereby became and is a valid first mortgage lien
upon the herein described real estate, subject only to the lien of the Treasurer for taxes; that said
conditions in the Mortgage have been broken, and the same has become absolute and the Plaintiff
is entitled to have the equity of redemption and dower of the Defendants who may have an interest
in and to the Property foreclosed.
Plaintiff has and will from time to time advance sums for taxes, insurance and the
Property’s protection. Plaintiff has a lien for these amounts in addition to the amount set forth
above. The Court makes no finding as to the amounts of the advances and continues same until
the confirmation of sale.
There is no just cause for delay.
It is therefore ORDERED, ADJUDGED, AND DECREED that unless the sums
hereinabove found due, together with the costs of this action, be fully paid within three (3) days
from the date of the entry of this decree, the equity of redemption and dower of Defendants with
an interest in and to the said real estate shall be foreclosed, and the said real estate sold, that the
Plaintiff may issue an order of sale directed to the Sheriff of Montgomery County, directing him
to appraise, advertise in a paper of general circulation within the County, and sell the said real
estate as upon execution and according to law, free and clear of the interest of all parties to this
action. The United States of America shall have the right to redeem as provided by Title 28,
Section 2410 (C), United States Code.
This is a final appealable order.
SO ORDERED.
