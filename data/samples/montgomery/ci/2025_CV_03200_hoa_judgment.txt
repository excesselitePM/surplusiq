This cause was submitted to the Court and heard upon the Complaint of the
Plaintiff; the Answer and Disclaimer of Interest of Defendant, State of Ohio
Department of Taxation; the Answer of Defendant, Treasurer of Montgomery County;
the Answer of Substitute Defendant, PennyMac Loan Services, LLC; the Plaintiff's
Motion for Default Judgment and the evidence. The Court further finds that
Plaintiff's Motion for Default Judgment is well taken and the same is hereby granted.
The Court finds that all necessary parties have been served with summons
according to law and are properly before the Court.
The Court further finds Defendant(s), Jeremy Dee Harper and Sothea C.
Harper, are in default of Answer or other pleading as to Plaintiff’s Complaint and
thereby admit all of the allegations of Plaintiff’s Complaint.
The Court finds that there is due the Treasurer of Montgomery County, Ohio,
accrued real property taxes, penalties, and interest thereon, upon the premises
hereinafter described, the exact amount being unascertainable at the present time,
but which amount will be determined at the time of sale of said premises, for which
amount the Montgomery County Treasurer has a good and valid lien.
The Court further finds that there is due Substitute Defendant, PennyMac
Loan Services, LLC, on the promissory note set forth in its Answer, an amount to be
determined by further order of this Court.
The Court further finds that in order to secure the indebtedness evidenced by
the promissory notes, a certain mortgage deed was executed and delivered securing
the following premises:
Premises Known As: 6903 Greeley Avenue
Dayton, Ohio 45424
Permanent Parcel No. R72610815 0003
(SEE LEGAL DESCRIPTION ATTACHED HERETO AS EXHIBIT “A”)
The Court finds that the assignment of mortgage was duly filed with the
Montgomery County Recorder on July 18, 2025, in Instrument No. 2025-00037523;
and thereby became the first lien upon said premises, subject only to the lien of the
Treasurer for taxes.
The Court further finds that the mortgage of Substitute Defendant, PennyMac
Loan Services, LLC, is a prior lien as defined in O.R.C. Section 2329.20 and that the
decree rendered herein will not affect said mortgage or obligation secured thereby
and that said property is to be sold subject to the mortgage of PennyMac Loan
Services, LLC.
The Court further finds that there is due to Plaintiff, the sum of $5,986.35 plus
interest at 8% pursuant to O.R.C. §1343.01, from July 22, 2025, on the Certificate of
Continuing Lien for delinquent maintenance fees and assessments as set forth in its
Complaint, which lien was filed for record as Instrument No. 2022-00001838 of
Montgomery County Records, and thereby became and is a good, valid and subsisting
lien upon the premises herein and, as a result, the Plaintiff is entitled to judgment
against Jeremy Dee Harper and Sothea C. Harper, jointly and severally, subject to
automatic subsequent adjustments reflecting any additional unpaid interest,
administrative late fees, enforcement assessments, collection costs, attorney’s fees,
paralegal fees, and court costs, and is entitled to foreclose on the subject property.
The Court finds there is no just reason for delay.
ORDER
It is therefore ORDERED, ADJUDGED AND DECREED that, unless the sums
hereinabove found due, together with the costs of this action, including the cost of the
judicial certificates of title filed herein for which the Plaintiff is entitled to
reimbursement, be fully paid prior to confirmation of sale, the equity of redemption
of all defendants in and to said premises shall be foreclosed.
Said premises shall be advertised for sale and sold pursuant to O.R.C. Section
2329.20 and Section 2329.21, subject to Defendant, PennyMac Loan Services, LLC 's
mortgage, that said premises shall be advertised for sale and sold at a minimum
value of not less than two-thirds of the difference between the value of the real estate
as appraised and the amount remaining unpaid on the note and mortgage of
Defendant, PennyMac Loan Services, LLC.
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED, ADJUDGED AND DECREED that the Sheriff,
upon confirmation of sale, shall pay from the proceeds thereof, the following:
FIRST: The costs herein, including the sum of $1,049.00 payable to
Plaintiff, c/o Kaman & Cusimano, LLC, for the Judicial Reports
filed herein.
SECOND: To the Treasurer of this County the unpaid taxes, assessments,
interest and penalties due and payable on said premises.
THIRD: To Plaintiff, the sum of $5,986.35 plus interest at 8% pursuant to
O.R.C. §1343.01, from July 22, 2025, on its lien and judgment,
subject to automatic subsequent adjustments reflecting any
additional unpaid interest, administrative late fees, enforcement
assessments, collection costs, attorney’s fees, paralegal fees, and
court costs, through the date of confirmation.
FOURTH: The balance of proceeds, if any, to be held by the Clerk of Courts
pending further order of court.
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED, ADJUDGED AND DECREED that after said
sale has been completed, the Sheriff of Montgomery County convey to the purchaser
of said real property and other items so sold by deed and provided by law, and that a
Writ of Possession of said property be issued.
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED, ADJUDGED AND DECREED that upon
distribution of the proceeds of sale as aforesaid, the Clerk of this Court shall issue his
certificate to the Montgomery County Recorder directing him to enter the same on
the margin of the records of said liens, releasing same from the premises.
RECORD IS HEREBY ORDERED.
