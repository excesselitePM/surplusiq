This matter is before the Court upon the Motion for Summary Judgment of the Plaintiff,
UMB Bank, National Association, not in its individual capacity, but solely as legal title trustee
for PRL Title Trust II, to obtain a finding of default upon the Note as described in the Complaint;
and to foreclose the lien of the Mortgage securing the obligation of the Note upon the real estate
described hereto; and to require all parties to set up their claims to the real estate or be forever
barred.
The Court finds that all necessary parties are properly before the Court; that the following
Defendants have been duly served with Summons and a copy of the Complaint, or by publication
with proof of the last date of publication filed with the Court, to wit:
 Michael Allen Nolte by Certified Mail on July 29, 2025
 Heartland of Kettering OH, LLC dba Heartland of Kettering by Process Server on
October 28, 2024
 State of Ohio Department of Taxation by Process Server on October 28, 2024
 Montgomery County Treasurer by Process Server on October 28, 2024
The Court finds that reasonable minds can come to but one conclusion, which is adverse
to the Defendant Michael Allen Nolte, that there exists no genuine issues of material fact and that
the Plaintiff is entitled to judgment in its favor as a matter of law.
The Court finds that the Defendant, Michael Allen Nolte has filed an Answer in response
to the Plaintiff's Complaint. The Court finds that the Plaintiff has filed a Motion for Summary
Judgment supported by a Memorandum and Affidavit. Upon consideration thereof, the Court
finds no genuine issues as to any material fact, and that the Plaintiff is entitled to a Judgment and
Decree in Foreclosure as a matter of law. Accordingly, Plaintiff's Motion for Summary Judgment
is hereby granted.
The Court further finds that the doctrine of Lis Pendens has attached to the real property,
which is the subject of this action on October 21, 2024. Accordingly, all liens and interests filed
for record after October 21, 2024, and all pending actions are forever barred.
The Court finds that the allegations contained in the Complaint are true, that there is due
and owing to the Plaintiff, on the Note, the sum of $1,716.87, which consists of an interest
bearing principal balance of $1,604.69 and a non-interest bearing deferred principal balance of
$112.18, plus interest at the rate of 8.50900 percent per annum from October 3, 2022 together
with late charges, advances for the protection and maintenance of the property, condominium
association or homeowners association dues and penalties and costs.
The Court finds that the Note was secured by the Mortgage held by Plaintiff which,
except for property taxes or assessments, constitutes a valid and subsisting lien upon the subject
property commonly known as 130 W Melford Ave, Dayton, OH 45405, and more particularly
described in Exhibit ‘A’ hereto. The Court further finds that the subject mortgage was recorded
on October 18, 2002, in Instrument 02-128445, of the records of the Montgomery County
Recorder; that the Mortgage was subsequently assigned to Plaintiff and that the conditions of
said Mortgage have been broken and the Plaintiff is entitled to have the equity of redemption of
the Defendant-titleholders foreclosed.
The Court finds that the Defendant, Montgomery County Treasurer, has filed an answer
or appearance herein, and has or may have an interest in the subject property, the exact amount
which cannot be ascertained at this time, but will be known upon the sale of the subject property.
This interest is the first and best lien upon the subject property, senior in priority to all other
liens, and will be protected accordingly.
The Court finds that the defendant, Heartland of Kettering OH, LLC dba Heartland of
Kettering , has filed an answer or appearance herein asserting an interest in the subject property,
the exact amount which cannot be ascertained at this time, but will be known upon the sale of the
subject property. This lien interest is subordinate and inferior to the lien of the Plaintiff.
The Court finds that the defendant, State of Ohio Department of Taxation, has filed an
answer or appearance herein asserting an interest in the subject property, the exact amount which
cannot be ascertained at this time, but will be known upon the sale of the subject property. This
lien interest is subordinate and inferior to the lien of the Plaintiff.
IT IS THEREFORE ORDERED, ADJUDGED AND DECREED
Plaintiff is entitled to a finding of default upon the Note and IN REM judgment for the
sums set forth above.
Plaintiff is entitled to a decree of foreclosure on the Mortgage filed October 18, 2002, in
Instrument 02-128445, of the records of the Montgomery County Recorder. Unless there is paid
or caused to be paid by the Defendant(s) within five (5) days of the date of this entry: (i) to the
Clerk of this Court the costs of this action; and (ii) to the Plaintiff, the sums found to be due and
owing to the Plaintiff as set forth above, the equity of redemption of any and all defendants and
all persons claiming under and through them in said Real Property are forever barred and
foreclosed; and an Order of Sale shall issue to the Sheriff of this County, directing said Sheriff to
appraise, advertise and sell said real property according to the law and orders of this Court, and
to report the results of the sale proceedings to this Court.
That the Sheriff shall send counsel for the party requesting the Order of Sale a copy of the
publication notice promptly upon its first publication.
That the Sheriff shall pay from the proceeds of said sale, upon the claims herein found,
the amounts thereof in the following order of priority:
1. To the Clerk of this Court, the costs of this action including the fees of appraisers.
2. To the Treasurer of this County, the taxes and assessments, due and owing as of the
date of transfer of the property after Sheriff’s Sale.
3. To the Plaintiff, the sum of $1,716.87, which consists of an interest bearing principal
balance of $1,604.69 and a non-interest bearing deferred principal balance of $112.18, plus
interest at the rate of 8.50900 percent per annum from October 3, 2022, together with all
expenses, together with advances made for protection and maintenance of the property,
condominium association or homeowners association dues and penalties, and costs.
4. The balance of the sale proceeds, if any shall be held by the Sheriff to await further
Order of this Court.
THIS IS A FINAL APPEALABLE ORDER. PURSUANT TO APP.R.4, THESE
PARTIES SHALL FILE A NOTICE OF APPEAL WITHIN THIRTY (30) DAYS.
