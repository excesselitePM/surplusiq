This cause was submitted to the Court and heard upon the Complaint of the
Plaintiff; the Answer of Defendant, Montgomery County Treasurer; the Answer of
Defendant, Adair 2000 with Union Bank as Secured Party; the Plaintiff's Motion for
Default Judgment and the evidence. The Court further finds that Plaintiff’s Motion
for Default Judgment is well taken and the same are hereby granted.
The Court finds that all necessary parties have been served with summons
according to law and are properly before the Court.
The Court further finds Defendant(s), Moh Fardeen Ahmadi and Unknown
Spouse, if any of Moh Fardeen Ahmadi, are in default of Answer or other pleading as
to Plaintiff’s Complaint and thereby admit all of the allegations of Plaintiff’s
Complaint.
The Court finds that there is due the Treasurer of Montgomery County, Ohio,
accrued real property taxes, penalties, and interest thereon, upon the premises
hereinafter described, the exact amount being unascertainable at the present time,
but which amount will be determined at the time of sale of said premises, for which
amount the Treasurer of Montgomery County, Ohio, has a good and valid lien.
The Court further finds that there is due the Defendant, Adair 2000 with
Union Bank as Secured Party, on its tax lien certificates as set forth in its Answer,
an amount to be determined by further order of this Court.
The Court further finds that there is due to the Plaintiff, the sum of $9,155.35
with interest at the maximum rate of 8% per annum from June 2, 2025, for delinquent
maintenance fees and assessments as set forth in its Complaint, which lien is filed
for record as Instrument No. 2024-00026968 of Montgomery County Records, and
thereby became and is good, valid and subsisting lien upon the premises herein and,
as a result, the Plaintiff is entitled to personal judgment against Moh Fardeen
Ahmadi, subject to automatic subsequent adjustments reflecting any additional
unpaid interest, administrative late fees, enforcement assessments, collection costs,
attorney’s fees, paralegal fees, and court costs, and is entitled to foreclose on the
subject property.

The Court further finds that there is no just reason for delay in entering

ORDER
It is therefore ORDERED, ADJUDGED AND DECREED that, unless the sums
hereinabove found due, together with the costs of this action, including the cost of the
judicial certificates of title filed herein for which the Plaintiff is entitled to
reimbursement, be fully paid prior to confirmation of sale, the equity of redemption
of all defendants in and to said premises shall be foreclosed.
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED, ADJUDGED AND DECREED that the Sheriff,
upon confirmation of sale, shall pay from the proceeds thereof, the following:
FIRST: The costs herein, including the sum of $866.00 payable to
Plaintiff, c/o Kaman & Cusimano, LLC, for the Judicial Reports
filed herein.
SECOND: To the Treasurer of this County the unpaid taxes, assessments,
interest and penalties due and payable on said premises.
THIRD: To the Defendant, Adair 2000 with Union Bank as Secured Party,
an amount to be determined by further order of this Court.
FOURTH: To Plaintiff, the sum of $9,155.35 with interest at the maximum
rate of 8% per annum from June 2, 2025, on its judgment and
condominium lien, subject to automatic subsequent adjustments
reflecting any additional unpaid interest, administrative late
fees, enforcement assessments, collection costs, attorney’s fees,
paralegal fees, and court costs, through the date of confirmation.
FIFTH: The balance, if any to be held by the Clerk of Court pending
further order of the Court.
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED, ADJUDGED AND DECREED that after said
sale has been completed, the Sheriff of Montgomery County convey to the purchaser
of said real property and other items so sold by deed and provided by law, and that a
Writ of Possession of said property be issued.
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED, ADJUDGED AND DECREED that upon
distribution of the proceeds of sale as aforesaid, the Clerk of this Court shall issue his
certificate to the Montgomery County Recorder directing him to enter the same on
the margin of the records of said liens, releasing same from the premises.
RECORD IS HEREBY ORDERED.
